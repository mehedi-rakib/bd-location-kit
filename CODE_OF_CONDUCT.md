# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in the BD Location Kit project and its communities will experience an environment free of harassment and discrimination.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Being helpful and supportive

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination based on identity or characteristics
- Aggressive or insulting language
- Unwelcome advances or comments of a sexual nature
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any other conduct that violates professional standards

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, or to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community. This includes:

- Using an official project email address
- Posting via an official social media account
- Representing the project at online or offline events
- Other conduct specifically defined and ruled on by maintainers

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers:

- **Email:** `mehedirakib.dev@gmail.com`
- **Subject:** `[CODE OF CONDUCT] Incident Report`

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. The project maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

### Enforcement Process

1. **Report received** - Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours
2. **Investigation** - Gather information and context
3. **Decision** - Determine appropriate action
4. **Communication** - Inform all parties of decision
5. **Follow-up** - Monitor for compliance

### Consequences

Depending on the severity, consequences may include:

- Warning
- Temporary muting of communications
- Temporary ban from project
- Permanent ban from project

## Appeal Process

Anyone who believes they have been treated unfairly can appeal by emailing:
- **Email:** `mehedirakib.dev@gmail.com`
- **Subject:** `[CODE OF CONDUCT] Appeal - Case #[number]`

Appeals will be reviewed by an independent party within 7 days.

## Community Guidelines

### Be Respectful

- Treat all community members with respect
- Assume good intentions
- Address disagreements professionally

### Be Inclusive

- Welcome diverse perspectives
- Support underrepresented groups
- Use inclusive language

### Be Constructive

- Provide helpful feedback
- Explain your reasoning
- Offer solutions, not just criticism

### Be Transparent

- Be honest in your communications
- Admit mistakes
- Acknowledge limitations

## Examples in Practice

### Good Examples

```
"Great work on that feature! I have a suggestion that might 
improve performance. Have you considered using a Map instead 
of an Object for the lookup?"
```

```
"I disagree with this approach. Here's why I think X might 
work better: [reasoning]. What do you think?"
```

```
"I'm new to this project and would love to contribute. 
Could someone point me to good first issues?"
```

### Bad Examples

```
"This code is terrible. How did this even get merged?"
```

```
"You obviously don't know what you're doing."
```

```
"This project is for [specific group] only."
```
